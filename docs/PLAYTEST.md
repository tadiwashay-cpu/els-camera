# Playtest guide (v0.8.0: toon look, bigger maps, wall slams)

## Install
1. On the tablet open GitHub > this repo > **Releases** > "Block Duelist 3D
   v0.8.0 (playtest)" and download the APK (or Actions > latest run > Artifacts).
2. Tap it, allow "install unknown apps", open **Block Duelist 3D**.
3. Settings: Graphics on **MEDIUM** for the gates, turn **Show FPS** on.

## What to play

| # | Do this | Look for |
|---|---------|----------|
| T1 | Look at the touch buttons in a match, pick up different weapons | Each button's picture says what it does? ATTACK changes with the weapon? |
| T2 | Play every arena | The toon look (bands of light, outlines) is the style you wanted? Try Settings > Art style > Classic to compare |
| T3 | Walk on sand, rocks, the wreck deck, the harbor | Real textures add grain without looking messy? |
| T4 | Beach Island: walk the pier east to Palm Cove; climb the lookout; take the pad back | Feels like a real island? Palms and rocks look good? Bots follow you there? |
| T5 | Harbor Factory: go west through the container wall | Rail Yard fun to fight in? Tower and pyramid reachable? |
| T6 | Heavy-hit bots into walls, get slammed yourself | Slams feel real (thud, dust, damage)? Too strong / too weak? |
| T7 | 8 bots on the bigger maps, Medium graphics, Show FPS on | FPS still fine? Where does it drop? |
| N1 | First person (TPP button top right): look around, run, fight with a sword, hammer, spear, gun, bow, shield | No eyes/mouth/body. Weapon floats bottom right; swings cross the screen when they hit? Too big / too small? |
| N2 | First person: whip the view round fast, sprint | Motion blur feels like a real eye, or too much? (Settings > FPP motion blur) |
| N3 | First person: walk into a wall | The weapon lifts away instead of poking through |
| N4 | Use weapon powers: Thunderbolt, Magic Scepter, Plasma Blade, Ice Cream, Wrench, Lollipop, Rubber Chicken, Ship Anchor | New casting moves (sky call, kneel and strike the ground, power-up burst, push, pull) look good? |
| N5 | Title > LOCKER > LOOK: try faces, hair, hats, colours; buy one | Your fighter changes beside the menu; you look like that in a match and on the loading screen |
| N6 | LOCKER > UPGRADES: buy a level or two, play | You feel faster / tougher? Prices fair? |
| N7 | Walk through every menu | New look (slanted buttons, yellow stripes, bold italic letters) feels like the style you wanted? |
| N8 | ARMORY > pick a weapon > INSPECT, drag on the right side | The weapon turns under your finger |
| N9 | Two phones, LAN, different looks and upgrades | Each phone shows everyone's look; nothing out of sync |
| F1 | In a match tap TPP (top right) to go first person; fight, throw, jump | Feels like CODM's FPP? |
| F2 | Settings > CONTROLS > EDIT TOUCH LAYOUT: move and resize buttons, SAVE, play | Buttons where you put them, still easy to hit |
| F3 | Watch fights | Life bars over heads readable at a distance? No number pop-ups, no power names |
| F4 | Harbor Factory, 8 bots, start several matches | Nobody starts in the water or stacked |
| A | Play each arena on MEDIUM, then HIGH | Lighting, haze, shiny metal look better? FPS still fine (show FPS on)? |
| B | Get hit, land heavy hits, get a kill, dash | Red edge flash, jolts, speed blur: good or too much? |
| C | Run sideways, jump off something high | Camera lean and landing dip feel good, not sick-making? |
| D | Step on the glowing bounce pads (yard edges, beach, harbor) | You fly to the island / rock / barge / roof and land safely |
| 0 | Title > ARMORY, tap through the weapons; then play with a few | Curved models look good up close and in a fight? Anything looking wrong in a hand? |
| 0b | Buy the Shield (A rank), play with it | Big round shield; bash, backhand, slam (charge), block and parry look right? |
| 1 | Title > ARMORY | 500 welcome coins; weapons by rank with prices; icons readable; the fighter holds the selected weapon |
| 2 | Buy a C or B weapon, EQUIP it, PLAY with "Start with: Your weapon" | You spawn with it every life |
| 3 | In a match, stand on a weapon you don't own | "LOCKED" with its price; you can't pick it up or catch it |
| 4 | Press SKILL (button above the attack buttons) with different weapons | Each power does something different; the weapon glows when it's ready |
| 5 | Try THROW with a hammer, bazooka or shield | Nothing happens: those can't be thrown |
| 6 | 8 bots, Beach Island, 3 min | Still playable? FPS? Spawns not stacked? |
| 7 | Stand still while a bot throws at you | "!" over your head, a chevron pointing at it, a tingle sound |
| 8 | Run, guard, swing; watch the arms | Hands stay outside the body |
| 9 | Jump and double jump | Higher than before, not floaty? |
| 10 | Results screen | More coins than before (15 a kill, 75 a win) |
| 11 | Two phones, LAN (Phase 3 gate) | Each phone starts with its own equipped weapon; powers show on both |
| 12 | Random arena, 5 min, 3 bots, Hard, Medium graphics (Phase 2 gate) | Near 60 fps, nothing stuck |
