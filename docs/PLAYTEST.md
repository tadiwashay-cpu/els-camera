# Playtest guide (v0.1.0)

## Install
1. On the tablet open GitHub > this repo > **Releases** > "Block Duelist 3D
   v0.1.0 (playtest)" and download the APK (or Actions > latest run > Artifacts).
2. Tap it, allow "install unknown apps", open **Block Duelist 3D**.
3. Settings: leave Graphics on MEDIUM, turn **Show FPS** on.

## What to play
| # | Do this | Look for |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | Play > 3 min, 1 bot, Normal | Does moving feel quick and responsive? Camera OK? |
| 2 | Tap ATTACK 3 times close to the bot | Do the 3 slashes chain? Do hits land when they should? |
| 3 | Hold ATTACK, release | Charged spin: does it feel powerful? |
| 4 | THROW your sword at the bot from 8 m | Does it fly where you meant? Easy to pick up a sword again? |
| 5 | Let the bot throw at you; press THROW as it arrives | Can you catch it? Is the timing fair? |
| 6 | Get knocked down / killed | Ragdoll looks fun, not broken? Killcam readable? Respawn quick enough? |
| 7 | Knock the bot off the edge | Does it count as your kill? |
| 8 | 5 min, 3 bots, Hard | Frame rate (bottom-left), heat, anything stuck or broken |
