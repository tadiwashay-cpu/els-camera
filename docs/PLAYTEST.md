# Playtest guide (v0.2.1: Phase 2 gate)

## Install
1. On the tablet open GitHub > this repo > **Releases** > "Block Duelist 3D
   v0.2.1 (playtest)" and download the APK (or Actions > latest run > Artifacts).
2. Tap it, allow "install unknown apps", open **Block Duelist 3D**.
3. Settings: Graphics on **MEDIUM** for the gate, turn **Show FPS** on.

## What to play
| # | Do this | Look for |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | Beach Island, 3 min, 1 bot | Does the island read well? Sand, water, palms, waterfall OK? |
| 2 | Stand in the orange ring by the wreck's cannon | Fired onto the lighthouse, land on top (not off the edge)? Fun? |
| 3 | Wait for 0:45 | "Tide coming in": beach floods, wading slower, recedes after ~25 s |
| 4 | Knock the bot off the pier end or the rope bridge | Shark grabs them, the kill is yours |
| 5 | Harbor Factory, 3 min, 2 bots | Dusk + rain look, FPS in the rain, warehouse lit enough? |
| 6 | Walk under the magnet holding a sword, then with fists | Grabbed with the sword (jump to kick free), ignored with fists |
| 7 | Stand on the yellow pad by the blue booth (quay) | You get the crane for 10 s: it hunts and dunks the bots |
| 8 | Run across an oil spill; throw a bomb on one | Slippery; it burns and hurts for 8 s |
| 9 | Watch the conveyor (inside the warehouse) | Crates crushed every 4 s; red light + alarm before each slam |
| 11 | Jump off the pier (Beach) or the quay (Harbor) | You swim, health drains slowly, JUMP at a ledge climbs out |
| 12 | Start with "Random weapon" a few times | New weapons: shield blocks, grapple pulls, fan pushes, bazooka, lightning |
| 10 | **Gate:** Random arena, **5 min, 3 bots, Hard, Medium graphics** | FPS stays near 60 (lowest?), nothing stuck or broken |
